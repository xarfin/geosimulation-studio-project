The study area

Our study focuses on Schiehaven Noord and its surroundings in Rotterdam. The gold line shows the study area and the pink line shows the development area.

Method and Sources

  • OpenStreetMap provides the street map.
  • PDOK / Beeldmateriaal Nederland provides the aerial photos. We cropped the 2026 image to our study boundary and saved it in the app.
  • The study-area boundary and development-area boundary are the GeoJSON files prepared for this project.
  • The municipality's Schiehaven Noord page gives background on the plans for the development area.
