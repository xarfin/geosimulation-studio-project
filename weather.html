Our project rule: show a monthly mean only if at least 80% of days have valid data for that variable. Otherwise show “Insufficient data”. Missing values are never replaced with zero. A single year is not a long-term climate normal.

Sources, interpretation and limits

Regional conditions. These observations come from Rotterdam Airport, not from sensors at our site. Buildings, vegetation and waterfront exposure can change pedestrian-level wind and local temperatures. Use the wind simulation to assess the effects of the designs.

Live readings. KNMI near-real-time 10-minute observations are provisional. Temperature is the preceding 1-minute mean at 1.5 m; wind speed is the preceding 10-minute mean representative of 10 m. Direction describes where wind comes from. The latest valid reading is shown separately for each variable, with its own time.

Historical summaries. Validated daily TG and FG are already in °C and m/s. Their timestamps describe the end of the preceding UTC day. We assign each value to that day before computing monthly means. Daily aggregation stays in UTC; live display times use Europe/Amsterdam.

Updates. The page polls every 10 minutes. The server caches live requests for 10 minutes and historical requests for 24 hours. “Delayed” means a valid observation is over 30 minutes old. On a failed update, retained values are clearly marked.
