Method and Sources

Accessibility

We analysed pedestrian accessibility from six residential buildings in Design B to five destinations: a supermarket, school, medical centre, tram stop and playground. The existing pedestrian network was compared with a proposed network containing additional conceptual pedestrian connections.

Using Python and NetworkX, Dijkstra's algorithm calculated the shortest walking paths through the connected network. Travel times were estimated at 80 metres per minute. Average walking distances were calculated across the six buildings and compared between both scenarios.

  • Overture Maps Transportation provided pedestrian network data.
  • PDOK supplied geographic reference layers.
  • Building locations and the development boundary were taken from the Design B project model.
