Source files and method

The supplied 1 m analysis uses a flat-ground observer at 1.5 m, 36 sky-view directions and a 150 m search radius. The H/W layer is a proximity proxy. Building heights come from the supplied footprints; floor-area estimates assume 3 m per floor.

Overlay colours and geographic rectangles are preserved from the source. Numeric rasters were not supplied; values shown here are the source's area summaries. Green share includes ground vegetation and tree canopy; green roofs are reported separately for the development site.
