Method and Sources

We placed the layers over the same satellite image and cropped the data to our study area. The 3D buildings use the same boundary, so buildings outside the image are hidden.

  • PDOK / Beeldmateriaal Nederland provides the satellite background. We used the 2026 aerial photos.
  • The study-area boundary and development-area boundary are the polygons prepared for this project.
  • BAG via PDOK provides the building footprints, including their construction year and use.
  • BGT via PDOK provides the green-space and water polygons.
  • AHN via PDOK provides the ground heights at 0.5 m resolution. We coloured these heights on a scale from -2 to 10 metres NAP.
  • Rotterdam in 3D provides the existing buildings in LoD2. We filled available gaps with 3DBAG LoD2.2 models (CC BY 4.0). The two sources are from different surveys, so some recent buildings may still be missing.
