Model and design connections

A separate LSTM with 32 units and one output is trained for each indicator. It learns seasonal anomalies from a 60-month window; longer forecasts reuse its previous predictions.

Historical testing uses training data through 2012 and validation in 2013–2015. The final forecast uses training through 2022 and validation in 2023–2025.

  • Temperature: shade, trees and green areas.
  • Rainfall: rain gardens, permeable surfaces and water storage.
  • Wind: test building layouts and outdoor comfort with local wind simulations.

The supplied KNMI’23 chart shows separate national scenarios for 2050, relative to 1991–2020. These are distinct from the LSTM output.
