Method and Sources

We divided the development area into 5-metre cells. A cellular automaton adds housing and green space step by step, using nearby cells, access, slope and distance from water. Scenario A gives more weight to public connections. Scenario B gives more space to a connected park. The rounds show the order of allocation.

Both scenarios aim for 600 homes. We assumed 75 m² per home, 80% usable floor area and 10 storeys. The blocks and trees are simple illustrations. Green roofs appear automatically in the 3D view and are counted separately from the park. The final figures describe the completed layout, including during playback.

  • The model settings and results record the rules, assumptions and checks. The existing-use grid, proposal, terrain and slope inputs were supplied with the project. Missing terrain values were filled from the nearest available cell.
  • Gemeente Rotterdam and the Schiehaven Noord ambitions document informed the housing, green-space and sports programme.
  • PDOK / Beeldmateriaal Nederland supplies the 2026 aerial photos used as the satellite background.
  • Rotterdam in 3D supplies the LoD2 buildings. Available gaps are filled with 3DBAG LoD2.2 models (CC BY 4.0). These are the same local models shown in Layers, cropped to the study boundary. For A and B, existing buildings inside the development area are hidden so the new layout is visible.
  • The study boundary and development boundary were prepared for this project. Tree positions and public paths are illustrative.
