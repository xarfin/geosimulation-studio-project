Method and Sources

To evaluate microclimatic wind conditions and environmental noise around Schiehaven Noord, we implemented numerical simulation workflows using OpenFOAM and NoiseModelling. The computational domains and baseline geometries were cropped to the study area to assess both local impacts and wider surrounding effects.

  • OpenFOAM (v2412) supplies the Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) steady-state RANS framework using the standard k-ϵ turbulence model for atmospheric boundary layer (ABL) flows. The reference wind speed is set to 10 m/s at a 10 m reference height with an urban roughness length (z0) of 1.0 m.
  • NoiseModelling (v6.0.0) calculates environmental road traffic noise propagation based on the European standard CNOSSOS-EU. Within this tool, roads extracted from OpenStreetMap serve as the primary noise sources to compute sound power levels (Lw) and receiver grids for evaluating acoustic shielding and noise shadows behind the urban blocks.
  • Rotterdam in 3D provides the detailed 3D building geometries and closed volumetric envelopes for the CFD simulation domain, supplemented where needed with data from the official 3D Rotterdam portal. Minor architectural details are simplified while preserving overall building heights and urban morphology.
  • OpenStreetMap (OSM), extracted via the BBBike tool, supplies the geographical network data, providing building polygons as obstacles for sound reflection and absorption alongside road features that are designated as noise sources in NoiseModelling.
  • PDOK / Beeldmateriaal Nederland supplies the 2026 aerial photographs used as the satellite background for spatial referencing.
  • The study boundary, development boundary, and the two proposed massing designs (Design A and Design B) were prepared specifically for this project to compare layout performance.
